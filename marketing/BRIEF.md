# VENTRA marketing brief (Workstream C)

Owner directive, 2026-11-09. Highest product priority stays Workstream A (AI Setter, AI Receptionist); the owner directs the
frontend (Workstream B). Marketing runs independently and never slows A.

## Goal
Fill the new VENTRA Instagram account, build an audience before launch, and prepare a funnel:
Instagram content → profile → landing page / waitlist → AI demo → qualified enquiry → customer.

## Audience
Service businesses (trades, clinics, gyms, salons, consultants, agencies) that lose work through slow replies, missed calls and
unbooked enquiries. Speak in outcomes: answered calls, booked jobs, faster replies, fewer no-shows. No AI jargon.

## Brand
Minimal, premium, futuristic. Purple, peach, white (see tokens in `docs/design/UI_BIBLE.md`). Ribbon-V logo at
`apps/web/public/brand/ventra-mark.png`. Outfit headlines, Hanken Grotesk body.

## Truth rules
No invented testimonials, numbers, customers or features. Product footage only from working labs/screens the coordinator confirms;
otherwise "Concept demonstration" is written on the frame. Industry statistics only with a cited, checkable source.

## Profile (proposed, for owner approval)
- Name: `VENTRA · AI Front Office`  (display-name keyword search: "AI receptionist", "AI setter")
- Handle: existing account (owner to confirm)
- Category: Software company  ·  Business account  ·  contact: email + website button (details from owner)
- Bio (150 chars max, 4 lines):
  `Your AI front office for service businesses`
  `📞 AI Receptionist answers & books calls`
  `💬 AI Setter replies, qualifies & books enquiries`
  `Launching soon · join the waitlist ↓`
- Link: waitlist / landing page when ready (owner provides; until then the website)
- Highlights (covers in brand style): Start Here · AI Setter · AI Receptionist · Demos · Updates

## First nine feed posts (carousels unless stated)
1. Introducing VENTRA — one platform, your AI front office; what it is in plain words; "built in Australia, launching soon".
2. Why businesses lose leads — the five leaks (slow reply, missed call, no follow-up, no booking link, after-hours silence).
3. Meet the AI Receptionist — answers, understands, books, transfers; the three operating modes explained simply.
4. Meet the AI Setter — replies to enquiries in minutes, asks the right questions, offers times, books, hands over when needed.
5. The cost of missed enquiries — a worked example with the reader's own numbers (calculator framing), no invented stats.
6. How AI books appointments — the journey: enquiry → questions → availability → confirmed booking → reminder.
7. Behind the scenes — real screenshots of the test labs and dashboard, honest "in development" labelling.
8. How VENTRA helps businesses grow — audit → fix the leaks → more booked work → time back; the Growth Audit as the entry point.
9. Join the waitlist / request a demo — single CTA, what happens next.

## First three Reels (≤ 45 s, captions burned in, CTA at the end)
1. "The call that didn't get answered" — a missed call at 5:02 pm, the enquiry goes elsewhere; the AI Receptionist version; concept demonstration label unless real call audio exists.
2. "From enquiry to booked in one conversation" — the Setter qualifying and offering times (lab footage when A-3 is running; concept until then).
3. "Building VENTRA" — founder-style build story: the problem, the Build Plan, the labs, honest progress.

## Cadence (30-day calendar)
3 feed posts, 2 Reels, 3–5 Stories per week. Pipeline: research → ideas → script → production → review → schedule → performance review.
Scheduling: Instagram native scheduling / Meta Business Suite first; no paid tools without approval.

## Approvals
Publishing, scheduling, DMs, ads and paid tools need the owner. Everything is prepared to a `review/` state first.
