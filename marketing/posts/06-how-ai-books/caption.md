## Hook
From "can I get a quote?" to a confirmed booking, without the back-and-forth. Here's how it works.

## Body
The booking journey VENTRA is being built around, in five plain steps:

1. Enquiry: a customer asks, by phone or by message, at any hour.
2. Questions: just enough to know the job fits (where, how big, when).
3. Availability: open times offered straight away.
4. Booked: the customer picks a time; the booking and their details are saved for you, or held for your approval if you choose.
5. Reminder: planned, not built yet. A reminder before the appointment.

Honest status: steps 1 to 4 run in sandbox testing today, with practice conversations and simulated calls. Nothing is live with customers yet.
