## Hook
What do missed enquiries cost you? Not an industry average. Your number.

## Body
Three steps, two minutes, your own numbers:

A: in a normal week, how many calls go unanswered or messages wait too long?
B: out of every 10 enquiries you answer well, how many become paid work?
C: what's a typical job worth to you?

A × B ÷ 10 × C = the work that may be slipping away each week. Multiply by 52 for a year.

Example only: 4 missed, 3 in 10 won, $400 a job = $480 a week. Your numbers will be different, and that's the point.
