## Hook
Growing a service business doesn't always mean more ads. Sometimes it means fewer leaks.

## Body
The path VENTRA is built around:

1. Audit: start with a Growth Audit, a guided check of your business that shows where enquiries slip through.
2. Fix the leaks: start with the biggest gap. Missed calls point to the AI Receptionist, slow replies to the AI Setter, and your audit answers carry over into the setup.
3. More booked work: that's the aim. Calls answered, replies that offer times, follow-ups that don't get forgotten.
4. Time back: less phone tag, less chasing quotes, more time on the job.

When VENTRA launches, every business starts with the Growth Audit. It's in development now, alongside the AI Receptionist and AI Setter in sandbox testing.
