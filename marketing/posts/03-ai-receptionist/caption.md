## Body
Meet the AI Receptionist, one half of VENTRA's AI front office.

It's built to:
· answer your business calls instead of voicemail
· understand what the caller wants: a booking, a change of time, a cancellation or a message
· check availability and book a time, or hand the call to you when a person is needed

You choose how much it does: Automatic (it books on its own), Owner approval (it asks you first) or Enquiry only (it takes the details for you).

Honest status: today it runs simulated calls in our test lab. It isn't answering real calls yet. The slides marked "Concept demonstration" show how it's designed to work.
