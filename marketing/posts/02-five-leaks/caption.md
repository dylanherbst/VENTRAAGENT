## Hook
Good businesses still lose work, often in the gap between an enquiry and a reply. Here's where to look.

## Body
Five places enquiries leak out of a service business, and one question to check each:

1. The slow reply: how long did your last reply take?
2. The missed call: how many calls went unanswered last week?
3. No follow-up: who follows up on a quote, and when?
4. No easy way to book: can someone book without calling you?
5. After-hours silence: what happens to an enquiry that arrives at 9 pm?

Pick the one that costs you most and fix that first. No software needed to spot them.
