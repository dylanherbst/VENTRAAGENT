# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Adults (initially Australia) who want an extra income stream but lack time, capital, experience or technical skills: full-time employees, students and beginners, creators who struggle to monetise, and existing side hustlers drowning in manual tasks. They arrive from short-form video, search or referrals, usually on a phone, curious but sceptical.

## Product Purpose
An AI-driven platform that helps a person research a low-cost digital-product business, generate real products, publish a branded storefront, take payments and automate routine marketing and fulfilment. Success: a genuine customer signs up, approves a researched opportunity, publishes a store, receives a real payment and has the file delivered automatically, without developers doing steps behind the scenes.

## Positioning
Not another AI writing tool: it executes the workflow end to end (research → real product files → working store → verified payment → automatic delivery → approved organic marketing) inside one guided journey, with the owner approving every external action.

## Operating Context
Public website (aiventra.com.au) whose job is converting visitors: hero with 3D seed-to-tree story, a two-click "Build My AI Business" flow (choose a model or "Choose for me" → working preview → Save & Launch with transparent pricing). Behind it, the Next.js app on the VENTRA codebase (Supabase auth, Stripe, multi-tenant storefronts at /s/<slug>).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Working today: workspace auth (VENTRA), opportunity scoring rubric v1, multi-tenant storefront renderer with a DEMO store, blueprint-based previews.
- Not built yet (must be labelled, never implied): Stripe checkout and Connect payouts, automatic file delivery, AI product generation, social posting, live subscriptions.
- Business models in scope: digital templates/planners, printable art, personalised digital products, niche marketing assets. Memberships later.
- Draft pricing hypotheses (to validate with pilots): Discover free, Launch A$79/mo, Autopilot A$149/mo, Scale A$249/mo.
- Undecided: final brand name (working name used on the site, set in one config), first pilot niche, first social channel.

## Brand Commitments
- Working brand name is a placeholder until legal/Stripe review; the user chose not to show "AiMoneyPrinter" publicly yet.
- Visual references are binding: the four seed → planted seed → lavender sprout → three-leaf luminous tree images (white/pearl field, lavender and peach internal glow, one fixed camera). The reference video supplies behaviour only (real 3D geometry reacting to pointer and scroll, pieces separating and re-forming), not its dark/yellow look.
- Copy from the website plan is binding: eyebrow "THE NEXT WAVE OF ONLINE BUSINESS", headline "The AI boom is here. Build a business from it.", primary CTA "Build My AI Business", secondary "Watch AI Build a Business", reassurance "Simple setup. Guided launch. You stay in control." Stage headlines per plan §04.

## Evidence on Hand
No customers, testimonials, earnings data or case studies exist yet. Do not fabricate any. Real demonstrable assets: the DEMO storefront (/s/slow-morning) and the blueprint preview generator.

## Product Principles
1. Show, don't promise: let visitors experience a real preview before any form.
2. Automation reduces work; it never guarantees income. No fabricated urgency, earnings or testimonials.
3. The owner stays in control: approvals for publishing, payments, spending.
4. Unfinished capabilities are labelled honestly.
5. Mobile-first and fast; the 3D story must never block the CTA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard path, skip-animation link, prefers-reduced-motion shows static stages, hero copy and CTAs render without JavaScript or WebGL.
