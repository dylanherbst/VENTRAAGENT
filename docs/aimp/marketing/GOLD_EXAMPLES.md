# Marketing content: quality bar and gold examples (20 Nov 2026)

Mock customer: **Wedding Planning Paper**. Planner A$14, Tracker A$9. Audience: couples planning a wedding on a budget.
These are hand-written examples of what approved output should read like. They set the bar the AI writer (and the template fallback) is checked against.
Rules the copy must follow: say only what the listing says (no result promises, no "best", no invented numbers), one idea per post, plain words, the product name once, the price only where it helps.

## What the current template writer gets wrong (checked on the demo campaign)
1. **Wrong product text.** The tracker's Pin and SEO description say "Wedding Planning Printable planner, a printable planner..." Copy about one product is reused for the other.
2. **Names repeated and run together.** Caption: "A look inside the Wedding Planning Printable planner Wedding Planning Printable planner, a printable planner..." No punctuation between the hook and the description.
3. **Pin headline too long for an image.** "New from Wedding Planning Paper: Wedding Planning..." is cut with "..." on the picture itself. Image headlines must be 6 words or fewer.
4. **Junk keywords.** "wedding planning wedding planning..." and "wedding planning printable planner pdf" pair the same words twice.
5. **Boards named after products,** e.g. "Wedding Planning Printable planner (wedding planning)". A board is a topic ("Budget wedding planning"), never a product.
6. **Every Pin sounds the same.** Same sentence structure, same "Find it at ... for A$14" ending. Six Pins in a row read as spam.
7. **No image is produced,** only a brief. A Pin cannot be posted without one.

## Gold examples

### Pinterest Pin (inspiration angle)
- Image headline (max 6 words): **Plan the wedding, not the spreadsheet**
- Title (max 100): **Budget wedding planner printable: every cost in one place**
- Description (max 500): Planning a wedding on a budget? This printable planner puts your budget, guest list and to-do list on a few clean pages you can print today. Download the PDF and start with page one. A$14.
- Alt text: Cover of the Wedding Planning Paper wedding planner printable, with a soft green border and the title in cream lettering.
- Board: **Budget wedding planning**
- Keywords: budget wedding planner, wedding planner printable, wedding checklist printable, wedding budget tracker

### Pinterest Pin (inside look)
- Image headline: **See what is inside**
- Title: **Wedding budget tracker printable: what each page does**
- Description: A look at the budget tracker: one page to list every cost, one to track what you have paid, one to compare quotes. Print it at home and keep it in a folder. A$9.
- Alt text: Two pages of the wedding budget tracker laid side by side, showing a cost table and a paid-to-date column.
- Board: **Wedding budget tools**

### Instagram caption (three variants, owner picks one)
1. Wedding budgets get messy fast. This planner keeps costs, guests and tasks on a few printable pages. PDF download, A$14. Link in bio.
2. Quotes in your inbox, receipts in your bag, numbers in your head? The tracker puts them in one place. A$9, print and start. Link in bio.
3. Saturday job: print the planner, write down the budget, tick the first three tasks. That is a good start. Link in bio.

### Short video script (25 to 35 seconds)
- Hook (on screen, first 2 seconds): "Planning a wedding on a budget?"
- Beat 1: Hold up the printed planner. "This is the planner I would start with."
- Beat 2: Flip to the budget page. "Every cost goes here, so you always know where you stand."
- Beat 3: Flip to the guest list and tasks. "Guests and to-dos are on the next pages."
- Beat 4: Show the price. "A$14, a PDF you can print today."
- Call to action: "Link in bio."

### SEO (planner page)
- Page title (max 60): **Wedding Planner Printable | Wedding Planning Paper**
- Meta description (max 160): Printable wedding planner for couples on a budget. Budget, guest list and to-do pages in one PDF. Download and print today.

## Checks to automate next (step 2)
- No product text from a different product; no title repeated twice in one caption.
- Image headline 6 words or fewer, never cut with "...".
- No keyword where one word repeats; 3 to 6 keywords.
- Boards are topics, not product names.
- No two Pins in one campaign share the same opening three words or the same ending.
- Every caption variant is different in structure, not just word order.
