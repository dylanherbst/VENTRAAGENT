## Typical features
16 colours + 4 modes is the mid-range norm; cheapest models are one fixed sunset shade. Remote is common; smart models add app, voice and music sync. 180 or 360 degree rotating head. Mostly USB powered. Warmer tone around 3000K. Projection size grows with distance from the wall.

## What reviews complain about (small review samples, not statistically robust)
Gets hot (some say too hot after 10 to 30 minutes), cheap builds failing within a month, remotes needing to be pointed at close range or not working, defective on arrival, flimsy fittings. Praise: colour, cosy look, brightness, great for photos. [kimola.com reports 155282 and 141411]

## Honest limits
Decor, not health: not light therapy and not a proven sleep aid (Popsci). Best in a dim room. Sources are 2022 to 2024 except the Kmart pages (current).

## How this shaped the setter
