 Why this works
 • Opportunity: positions AI as a business-building advantage.
 • Simplicity: communicates that the system handles complex setup work.
 • Ownership: the language “my business” helps visitors picture a concrete outcome.
 • Low friction: the secondary demo CTA reduces commitment for sceptical visitors.
 • Visual reinforcement: the floating seed grows into a plant as the product promise unfolds.



 Important positioning boundary
 Present automation as a way to reduce work, not a guaranteed money-making machine. Any income examples or customer
 claims must be real, verifiable and appropriately qualified.

 The click-to-conversion journey
 Do not send visitors straight into a long registration form. Let them experience a personalised business result
 before asking for signup or purchase.


  01 FIRST CLICK                 Build My AI Business
                                 Open a visually simple business selection: choose a business model or select “Choose for
                                 me.”



  02 SECOND CLICK                Show a working preview
                                 Generate a sample business: name, offer, website design, pricing direction and marketing
                                 assets. Use real output, not simulated claims.



  03 CONVERSION                  Save & Launch My Business
                                 Display the actual price, plan details, required connections and approvals. Then offer
                                 signup and checkout.




 What the visitor should feel
 A personalised result creates momentum and a sense of ownership. The real product demo then establishes
 credibility: “I can see what I would get if I continued.”

 Measurement plan
 • Hero views and primary/secondary CTA click-through rates
 • Business-model selections and AI recommendation requests
 • Demo starts, successful preview generations and preview completion
 • Signup conversion, paid conversion and time from landing to purchase
 • Mobile-vs-desktop behaviour and performance impact of the 3D hero

 Conversion principles
 • Curiosity: See what AI could build for you.
 • Ease: Let customers try the preview without lengthy forms.
 • Personalisation: Recommend an opportunity based on their goals, skills and budget.
 • Proof: Use genuine product demos and verified customer results.
 • Momentum: Show real generation progress and next steps.
 • Risk reduction: State clear pricing, cancellation terms and launch requirements.
 • Trust: Avoid fake timers, invented earnings and promises of effortless profit.

 Claude — implementation brief
 AIMONEYPRINTER / HIGH-CONVERSION HOMEPAGE REQUIREMENTS

 Our website must convert visitors into customers as quickly and simply as possible. We are not primarily selling AI
 software; we are presenting the opportunity to build and operate an online business using AI-powered systems.

 Copy requirements
 Eyebrow: THE NEXT WAVE OF ONLINE BUSINESS
 Headline: The AI boom is here. Build a business from it.
 Supporting copy: Turn an idea into an online business with AI building your offer, website and marketing - and helping
 automate the work that follows.
 Primary CTA: Build My AI Business
 Secondary CTA: Watch AI Build a Business
 Reassurance: Simple setup. Guided launch. You stay in control.

 Development requirements
 01 Make the primary CTA visible without scrolling.

 02 Position the 3D white seed alongside the hero on desktop and above the copy on mobile.

 03 Use large, legible, restrained typography and a dark charcoal primary button.

 04 Keep the secondary button visually subordinate.

 05 Open a simple business-selection flow from the primary CTA.

 06 Let visitors choose a model or ask AI for a recommendation.

 07 Show a real personalised business preview as soon as practical.

 08 After the preview, show “Save & Launch My Business” and transparent pricing.

 09 Use authentic demos and substantiated social proof.

 10 Preserve responsive design, performance, accessibility and reduced-motion support.

 11 Track impressions, CTA clicks, selections, preview starts, signups and purchases.


 Product principle: Emphasise opportunity, simplicity, ownership, automation and immediate value. Keep AI jargon and
 complex menus out of the sales path.
 Non-negotiable: No fabricated urgency, guaranteed earnings, unsupported testimonials or false automation claims. Any
 unfinished capabilities must be labelled accurately.

 Success condition: Visitors should understand the offer, believe the product may help them, and know what to
 click within five seconds.
